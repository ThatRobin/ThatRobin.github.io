# Retro64 Fabric Port

Retro64 is a groundbreaking Minecraft mod that brings the magic of Super Mario 64 directly into your Minecraft world! This mod runs an internal emulator for the classic game, allowing you to play as Mario, complete with his signature moves, abilities, and 3D platforming gameplay, right inside Minecraft.

## Features
- Fully playable Super Mario 64 experience within Minecraft
- Control Mario with authentic moves: triple jump, wall kick, ground pound, and more
- Seamless integration: switch between Minecraft and Mario gameplay
- Unique power-up synergy and secrets to discover

## Technologies Used
- Minecraft Fabric modding
- Embedded Super Mario 64 emulator (Lib64)
